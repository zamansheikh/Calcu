Calculator for Android

Math, the way you write it.

Calcu typesets fractions, roots and powers as you type, so the screen shows the sum the way it looks on paper. Then it gives you the answer in the form you want.

A calculator that keeps up.

Everyday sums stay one tap away. The harder ones are there when you need them.

½

Natural display

Fractions stack, exponents rise and roots get their bar while you type. Tap anywhere in the sum to edit it.

=

Three answer forms

Press = again to switch between the decimal, the exact fraction (or root, or multiple of π) and a rounded value.

√

Scientific keys

Powers, roots, logarithms to any base, absolute value, π and e, in two slim rows that stay out of the way.

∞

Huge numbers

999 to the power 99,999 has nearly 300,000 digits. Calcu answers it instead of saying Error.

M

Saved results

Hold a result to keep it. Later, paste back either the answer or the whole equation it came from.
